## What this means for the research repo

- vllm-exl3 at `d3cfd394` and the recipe's `exl3-thor.patch` are AGPL-3.0-only. Fetching and applying them from a script
  (as `fetch_sources.sh` and inferencekey's `bootstrap.sh` do) keeps this repo free of AGPL code. Vendoring the patch or
  the plugin source, or a modified copy of either, would make those files AGPL-3.0-only here as well.
- Serving the patched plugin over a network to other users brings AGPL section 13 (offer the modified source). inferencekey's
  `vllm-exl3/a100/README.md:108-109` already states this. Their runs were private benchmark pods behind an API key.
- Everything else in the serving stack (vLLM, ExLlamaV3, sfxnz, Diffbot's MIT recipe parts, the weights) is permissive
  (Apache-2.0 or MIT), with attribution and notice-retention duties.
